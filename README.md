# The Climate of a Street · 거리의 기후

Street form and summer heat in Seoul, read from AI-labelled street imagery and 723 sensors.

**Live site:** https://floweringwisdom.github.io/climate-of-a-street/ (English and 한국어)

By **Jisoo Han**, Urban Planning and Engineering, Yonsei University, 2026.
The research behind the site was a 2026 capstone project carried out by Jisoo Han and Minju Won, supervised by Prof. Bon Woo Koo.

## What is here
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`: the site (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step)
- `data/`: small derived tables behind the charts (type averages, 동-level shares and rates, model estimates). No raw sensor data and no Street View images.
- `img/`: pre-rendered street-type map layers (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)
